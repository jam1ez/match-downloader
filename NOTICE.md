Match Downloader includes the Worlds channel, division, date and calibration data adapted from OpenScout, robostem-foundation/openscout, commit c7a1dcfdadbc942eb791d7b7e31d36589fc6f6da. The included configuration was transformed from src/jumper/data/worldsConfig.js; 2026 V5RC HS calibration values were transcribed from src/jumper/services/worldsSyncOffsets.js. Original source: https://github.com/robostem-foundation/openscout

Match Downloader is distributed under GNU AGPL version 3 (see LICENSE). All application source is included. yt-dlp, Python, Node.js and FFmpeg are separate dependencies under their respective licenses. Event schedules and timestamps are retrieved from VEX Events, OpenScout and RoboSTEM Jumper; recording rights remain with their owners.

Bundled preview libraries: @vimeo/player 2.30.0 (MIT, web/vendor/VIMEO-LICENSE.md) and hls.js 1.6.16 (Apache-2.0, web/vendor/HLS-LICENSE). YouTube's IFrame API is loaded from its official host for online previews.
